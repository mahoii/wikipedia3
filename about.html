About

This blog explores the various ways in which artists are exploited within the music industry. From unfair contract terms to inadequate royalty payments, the blog aims to shed light on the challenges faced by musicians in their careers. The content is curated to inform readers about the systemic issues and advocate for fair treatment of artists.

Purpose

The purpose of this blog is to raise awareness about this topic to people looking to make a real change like activist groups, who can bring this topic to the forefront and push for meaningful reforms.
